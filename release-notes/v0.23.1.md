## Writer Voice Fixes

Two fixes to how JARVIS handles the writer's voice.

### Highlights

- **The voice profile left the repository.** `packages/agents/_shared/prompts/voice-profile.md` held personal content in a public repo. It is now gitignored and stays local, and `voice-profile.md.example` ships in its place. The example carries the structure and the one lesson that matters: never re-derive a voice profile from LLM-assisted text, because assisted output is *approved* output, not evidence of unaided voice.
- **Content review judges voice against the writer, not a checklist.** The `/review` agent had the voice profile in its own prompt, but its nested `evaluate_content` call only got the skill's generic rules. Now the evaluator gets the voice profile too, and `anti-patterns.md` no longer demands sentence fragments or limits phrasal verbs, rules the voice profile rejects.

### Tests

**2,810 collected.** New tests cover the canonical-include helper and the evaluator's voice-profile handling.

### Compatibility

- **Existing clones** keep their local `voice-profile.md`; nothing changes.
- **A fresh clone** falls back to the `.example` with a startup warning. Copy it to `voice-profile.md` and make it yours.
