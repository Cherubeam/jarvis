## Specialists hand back, verified live

A day of AON-01 fixes, each checked against the real system. A live test showed that specialists didn't hand out-of-scope requests back to JARVIS. One tool-description change fixed it, and a rerun confirmed the fix.

### Highlights

- **Specialists know whom to hand back to.** `hand_back_to_jarvis` now lists the other specialists with their one-line descriptions. Before the fix, a `substack_publisher` session asked for a cover image wrote the image prompt itself. In the rerun it handed back ("…the substack_image_creator's job"), and JARVIS delegated to `substack_image_creator`. Costs about 270 input tokens per specialist call. Narrow specialist agency (OWASP LLM06).
- **No 4,096-token caps for reasoning models.** `/daily-summary` (CLI and GUI) uses `models.default_max_tokens`, and the golden judge gets 16,000. Reasoning models spend part of the limit on thinking (see #67).
- **Memory importer dates undated decisions** as `≤<fact date>` instead of leaving an HTML-comment placeholder that Obsidian rendered as `****`.

### Removed

- `packages/skills/base.py` (`BaseSkill`, `SkillConfig`) had no caller outside its own tests. The roadmap item "skills don't send `temperature`" is closed by deletion. `import_skill_module` now lives once, in `packages/skills/registry.py`.
- The CLI's duplicate copy of the agent tool assembly. Slash commands still used it; they now call `apps/cli/session_factory.py` like the rest of the CLI.

### Live checks

- GUI vault-write approval (#94, first live check): `/daily-summary` showed the real diff and path on the approval card, and Approve wrote the daily note (human oversight, EU AI Act Art. 14).
- Golden `--evaluate` with the new judge limit: 15/15 passed, average 0.888, $0.20. Every verdict carries real judge reasoning.
- Specialist hand-back (#72): failed before the fix, passed after it.
- Not yet checked: `/daily-summary` on a reasoning model, and whether the memory importer follows the `≤` rule.

### Tests

**2,814 collected** (2,823 in v0.33.0: the deleted `BaseSkill` took 13 tests with it, and 4 new tests were added).
