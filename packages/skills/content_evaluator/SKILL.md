# Content Evaluator

## What This Does
Runs content through two evaluation lenses: a marketing strategist
debate (does this serve the audience?) and a busy subscriber test
(would someone stop scrolling for this?).

## When to Use This
- Before publishing blog posts or newsletter editions
- When content feels "done" but you're not sure it lands
- To pressure-test hooks and structure

## What to Expect
- Identification of where readers might disengage
- Specific quotes with "deletion moments"
- Marketing strategist perspective on positioning
- Busy subscriber verdict: would they finish reading?

## Boundaries
Does NOT provide grammar or spelling checks
Does NOT rewrite your content
Does NOT validate factual accuracy
Does NOT evaluate SEO

## How to Invoke
Provide the full content and target audience.
Specify if you want line-by-line or big-picture feedback.

## Evaluation Framework

### Lens 1: Marketing Strategist Debate
Two strategists argue over the content:
- **Advocate**: Defends the piece — what works, what resonates
- **Critic**: Challenges assumptions — what's missing, what falls flat
- **Verdict**: Consensus on whether this serves the audience

### Lens 2: Busy Subscriber Test
Simulates a reader who:
- Has 47 unread emails
- Gives you 8 seconds to earn their attention
- Will close the tab the moment interest drops

The test identifies:
- **Hook strength**: Would they keep reading past the first paragraph?
- **Deletion moments**: Exact points where attention would drop
- **Finish probability**: Honest odds they'd read to the end
