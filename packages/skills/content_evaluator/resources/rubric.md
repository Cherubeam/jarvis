# Content Evaluation Rubric

## Hook Quality (1-5)
1. Generic opening, no reason to continue
2. Mildly interesting but forgettable
3. Solid hook, clear promise to the reader
4. Compelling — creates genuine curiosity
5. Irresistible — would stop mid-scroll

## Structure (1-5)
1. Wall of text, no visual breaks
2. Some structure but hard to scan
3. Clear sections, logical flow
4. Easy to scan AND rewarding to read fully
5. Structure itself adds meaning

## Audience Fit (1-5)
1. Wrong audience entirely
2. Right audience, wrong depth
3. Good fit, predictable angle
4. Strong fit, fresh perspective
5. Makes the reader feel seen

## Engagement Sustain (1-5)
1. Interest dies after first paragraph
2. Peaks early, fades out
3. Steady but not gripping
4. Builds momentum throughout
5. Strongest at the end — leaves them wanting more
